How the map estimate works

Confirmed Map 1 and Map 2 selections take priority. Before the veto, the model uses each team’s recent first bans and picks, weighs recent matches more, and considers both possible acting orders. It requires at least five complete BO3 vetoes from each team in the same observed pool. That pool is inferred from recent vetoes; tournament rules may differ.

These are experimental model estimates, not calibrated win or betting probabilities. The headshot baseline combines map likelihood, the player’s headshots per round, and typical map length. Small map samples are pulled toward the player’s overall rate. Roster changes and player roles are not modeled.

Data coverage & refresh

History comes from BO3.gg’s public completed-match records. A series is counted only when the same source player ID has valid integer headshots on completed Maps 1 and 2. Missing statistics stay missing.

Requested player profiles are refreshed in batches of up to four every six hours. GitHub may delay scheduled jobs. New searches can request a fresh collection; source coverage and the job queue affect availability. Other players from those same maps are also saved, with their limited coverage marked.

PrizePicks lines are entered manually. There is no real-time headshot or automated PrizePicks feed. Model estimates and historical hit rates do not guarantee outcomes.
